# ⛵Mobile Wallet Export Log Guide

Welcome to the Flow Mobile Wallet Export Log Guide! This document is designed to assist users in exporting logs from the Flow Wallet app. Exporting logs can be a crucial step in troubleshooting issues, as it provides developers with detailed information about app performance and potential errors. By following this guide, you will learn how to easily export logs, enabling our development team to pinpoint and address any issues you may encounter. Your cooperation in this process helps us enhance the user experience and ensure the smooth operation of the Flow Wallet.

Follow the steps below to export logs from the Flow Mobile Wallet:

**Step 1:** Navigate to the **Settings** page and locate the **Developer Mode** section.

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252FPtB8BPNeSp91B806oHew%252FIMG_7233.PNG%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D19b4352d-188f-4017-8cf8-6fa67265b114&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=df160a00&sv=2)

**Step 2:** Toggle the switch to turn on **Developer Mode**.

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252FIqB9JyhjJZQtSLYGVP0g%252FIMG_7234.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D8ec8c6be-b6db-4669-a40f-021164a6e9c1&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=b1f4acf3&sv=2)

**Step 3: Export the Log**

* For **Android Users**: Click the **Export Log** button.

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252Fdi14iDkA4MUnXo05MyhN%252FScreenshot_20241016-121231.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dadd1cc3e-161e-4a55-93c5-9601e89c706e&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=4d3c1c0d&sv=2)

* For **iOS Users**: Tap the **Share Log File** button.

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252FMh1hLyNgJaO3TeJfv946%252FIMG_7235.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D7c950ef9-fcf5-42b2-b35a-6f925ddc1a1a&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=46c17199&sv=2)

Once you have exported the logs, you can share them with our support team for further analysis. Thank you for helping us improve the Flow Wallet!
