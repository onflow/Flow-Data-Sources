# 💾Extension Private Key and Seed Phrase Guide

Welcome to the Flow Wallet Extension guide! Your private key and seed phrase are essential components for managing and securing your digital assets. In this guide, we will show you how to easily locate your private key and seed phrase directly from the Settings page of the Flow Wallet Extension. Having this information at your fingertips ensures that you can recover your wallet and maintain full control over your funds. Let’s dive in and learn how to access these crucial details safely!

1. Open the Flow Wallet Extension and navigate to the Settings page. Select the "Account List" section to view your accounts.

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252FYHEmmoRLR0uy0uEQY4a5%252FScreen%2520Shot%25202024-10-24%2520at%25205.10.44%2520pm.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D5cacabfb-abf8-41aa-a0aa-bd4423d70559&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=7fa16bdf&sv=2)

2. Click on your main account to access its details.

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252FONK5Ovb1g9b77es5YhA2%252FScreen%2520Shot%25202024-10-24%2520at%25205.11.38%2520pm.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D53e70456-df80-4639-962f-c8662e372321&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=dde550ef&sv=2)

1. On the account page, you will see your private key and seed phrase (if you restored your account using the seed phrase).

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252FCBi2Bh1dcfQkw1vwMDf6%252FScreen%2520Shot%25202024-10-24%2520at%25205.11.46%2520pm.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D8e8b79c2-f011-4324-9f09-439b2d8ae49e&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=3ae24f07&sv=2)

1. After verifying your password, you can securely view your private key and seed phrase.

![](https://docs.wallet.flow.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2259229985-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FCqw72ZIu4wNI7q40hHbt%252Fuploads%252FvKFCh8KUvIHxT5RE9zBF%252FScreen%2520Shot%25202024-10-24%2520at%25205.12.01%2520pm.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dc208d686-63bc-4d39-8595-50f1fb2ceb86&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=966fc258&sv=2)

1. Save this infomation ensures that you can recover your wallet and maintain full control over your funds.
